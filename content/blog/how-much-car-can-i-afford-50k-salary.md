The 15% rule: car costs ≤ 15% of take-home pay.

## On $50K: $15K–$20K car

- **Take-home:** ~$3,200/month
- **Max car budget:** $480/month (all-in)
- **Car payment alone:** ~$350/month max
- **At 7% for 60 months:** ~$18,000 car

## All-in means all-in

$480 covers: payment + insurance + gas + maintenance. A $350 payment + $150 insurance = $500. Already over!
