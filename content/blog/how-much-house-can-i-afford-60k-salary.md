$60,000 is workable for homeownership — especially with FHA loans.

## The number: $210K–$235K

- **Gross monthly:** $5,000
- **Max housing (28%):** $1,400/month
- After tax/insurance/PMI (~$350): ~$1,050 for P&I
- **Home price at 6.5%:** ~$225,000

## Make it work on $60K

1. **FHA loan:** 3.5% down = $7,875 on $225K
2. **Down payment assistance:** Many states offer $10K+
3. **Look at:** Midwest, South, smaller metros
4. **Avoid:** Coasts, major metros
