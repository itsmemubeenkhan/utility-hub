Florida has **no state income tax**, but 2026 buyers face a new challenge: **homeowner insurance costs have doubled** in many areas.

## Florida buying power on $70K

- **Gross monthly:** $5,833
- **Front-end limit (28%):** $1,633/month
- **Florida property tax:** ~0.9% (lower than Texas)
- **Homeowner insurance:** $2,500–$4,000/year (rising fast!)

That $1,633/month buys roughly **$230k–$260k** after accounting for Florida's steep insurance.

## The Florida insurance crisis

This is the #1 factor for 2026 Florida buyers:

- Average premium up 40%+ since 2022
- Some insurers leaving the state entirely
- Flood insurance often required separately
- Budget **$250–$350/month** for insurance alone

## Best Florida metros for $70K

- **Jacksonville:** Median ~$300k - possible in suburbs
- **Tampa:** Median ~$380k - tough on $70K
- **Orlando:** Median ~$390k - stretch
- **Miami:** Median ~$550k - out of range

## The math

- Max housing: $1,633/month
- Property tax (~$200 on $250k)
- Insurance (~$275 - Florida reality)
- PMI if <20% down (~$120)
- **Available for P&I:** ~$1,038/month
- **Home price:** ~$246,000
